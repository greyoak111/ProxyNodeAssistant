9 KM · MOD

Good field habits are part of the route.

  1. Carry enough water to avoid improvising at fragile sources.
  2. Keep to durable surfaces near wet ground and alpine plants.
  3. Record hazards accurately, without turning quiet places into spectacles.
  4. Turn around early when weather or pace changes the plan.
